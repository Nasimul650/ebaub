# EBAUB Digital Campus — Feature & Scope Specification

## 1. Purpose

This document defines what will and will not be built for the 5-day anniversary prototype.

The objective is a polished, convincing prototype — not a complete university ERP, LMS, or production information system.

---

# 2. Scope Categories

- MUST HAVE — required for the demonstration
- SHOULD HAVE — add if time allows
- FUTURE — after the presentation
- OUT OF SCOPE — do not implement in this prototype

---

# 3. Public Website — MUST HAVE

## Homepage

Include:

- Hero
- Primary CTA
- University introduction
- Academic programs
- Faculties/departments
- News
- Notices
- Events
- Campus/student-life content
- Verified university highlights
- Contact/footer

## Main Navigation

Core areas:

- About
- Academics
- Admissions
- Research
- Campus Life
- News
- Notices
- Events
- Contact

Every implemented navigation item must lead somewhere useful.

## Content Pages

MUST HAVE:

- Programs
- Departments
- Faculty
- News
- Notices
- Events
- Admissions
- Contact

SHOULD HAVE:

- Research
- Student life
- About sections
- Program detail pages

---

# 4. Public Search — SHOULD HAVE

A global search experience that can find:

- Programs
- Departments
- Faculty
- News
- Notices
- Events
- Pages

The search should prioritize useful information instead of acting as a generic website search box.

---

# 5. Public AI Assistant — MUST HAVE

Demonstrate an AI assistant that helps visitors discover university information.

Example questions:

- What programs does EBAUB offer?
- How do I find admission information?
- What departments are available?
- Show recent notices.
- Where is the university located?

For the prototype, the assistant can use a controlled knowledge source.

FUTURE:

- Full RAG across all CMS data
- Citations
- Multilingual support
- Advanced admission guidance

---

# 6. Student Dashboard — MUST HAVE

Initial sections:

- Dashboard
- Study
- Files
- AI
- Notices

The student dashboard is intentionally lightweight.

---

# 7. Student AI — MUST HAVE

At least one useful study demonstration.

Preferred flow:

```text
Student asks a study question
        ↓
AI explains the concept
        ↓
Student can ask a follow-up
```

Alternative:

```text
Study material
    ↓
AI summary
    ↓
Practice questions
```

Choose whichever is more reliable during implementation.

---

# 8. Teacher Dashboard — MUST HAVE

Initial sections:

- Dashboard
- Materials
- Teaching
- AI

The dashboard should communicate the direction of a future teacher portal.

---

# 9. Teacher AI — MUST HAVE

Recommended centerpiece:

## AI Quiz / Question Generator

Flow:

```text
Teacher selects topic
        ↓
Sets number/difficulty
        ↓
AI generates questions
        ↓
Teacher reviews/edits
        ↓
Teacher publishes
```

The teacher must remain the final decision-maker.

---

# 10. Teacher Materials — SHOULD HAVE

- Upload material
- List materials
- Basic categorization
- Publish/unpublish state

No full LMS material-management system is required.

---

# 11. Custom CMS — MUST HAVE

Admin/editor can manage:

- Pages
- News
- Notices
- Events
- Programs
- Faculties
- Departments
- Faculty members
- Media
- Announcements

Required CMS operations:

- Create
- Read
- Update
- Delete
- Publish/unpublish where appropriate
- Upload/select media

---

# 12. CMS Dashboard — SHOULD HAVE

Display:

- Content counts
- Recent content
- Draft/published counts
- Quick actions

Do not spend large amounts of time on advanced analytics.

---

# 13. Authentication — MUST HAVE

Provide:

- Login
- Logout
- Role-aware routing

Initial roles:

- ADMIN
- EDITOR
- TEACHER
- STUDENT

Admin/editor authentication is the highest priority because of the CMS.

---

# 14. Roles — MUST HAVE

ADMIN:
- Full CMS control

EDITOR:
- Public content management

TEACHER:
- Teacher dashboard

STUDENT:
- Student dashboard

Keep permissions simple during the prototype.

---

# 15. ToolMind — OUT OF CURRENT SCOPE

Do not integrate ToolMind during this 5-day prototype.

ToolMind remains a separate project and keeps its original product vision.

Possible future integration may include:

- PDF tools
- Document tools
- Image tools
- AI productivity tools

That comes after the anniversary project.

---

# 16. Explicitly OUT OF SCOPE

Do not attempt:

- Payment gateway
- Tuition collection
- Payroll
- Accounting
- Full ERP
- Complete LMS
- Full admission backend
- Real exam management
- Complex attendance
- Hostel management
- Transport management
- Advanced HR
- Full library-management platform
- Production-grade enterprise RAG
- Microservices
- Distributed realtime infrastructure

---

# 17. Prototype Data

The CMS is the source of truth for public content.

During the prototype:

- Seed data may be used
- Trusted classmates/friends may enter content
- Official data should be used where available
- Demo content must be clearly replaceable before production

---

# 18. Presentation Stories

## Story A — Visitor

```text
Visitor lands on EBAUB
        ↓
Finds information quickly
        ↓
Explores program / department
        ↓
Reads news / notice
        ↓
Uses AI assistant if needed
```

## Story B — Student

```text
Student logs in
        ↓
Student dashboard
        ↓
Study / Files / Notices
        ↓
AI study assistance
```

## Story C — Teacher

```text
Teacher logs in
        ↓
Teacher dashboard
        ↓
Materials / Teaching
        ↓
AI generates questions
        ↓
Teacher reviews
```

These stories should determine development priorities.

---

# 20. Scope Protection Rule

Every new feature request must be classified immediately:

- MUST HAVE
- SHOULD HAVE
- FUTURE
- OUT OF SCOPE

Do not allow late ideas to compromise the main presentation flow.

A polished prototype is more valuable than a larger unfinished one.

---

# 21. Definition of Done

The prototype is ready when:

- Public homepage feels substantially better than the existing website
- Main navigation works
- Important public pages work
- CMS can manage core content
- Admin/editor authentication works
- Student dashboard exists
- Teacher dashboard exists
- Public AI demo works
- Student AI demo works
- Teacher AI demo works
- Search works at a useful prototype level
- Mobile layout is acceptable
- No major internal links are broken
- Main visitor/student/teacher demo stories can be demonstrated reliably
