==================================================
PROJECT CONTEXT
==================================================

This is a 5-day prototype for the CSE Department's 2-year anniversary presentation at EXIM Bank Agricultural University Bangladesh (EBAUB).

The audience includes:
- CSE teachers and judges
- University administration
- Teachers from other departments
- Other ceremony attendees

The prototype has TWO major parts:

PART A — Modern Public University Website
PART B — Basic Digital Campus for Students and Teachers

The purpose is NOT to build a complete university ERP/LMS.

The current university website has problems including:
- Old visual design
- Poor UX
- Weak information hierarchy
- Some broken/unreliable links
- Outdated/irrelevant information
- Poor maintenance
- Information-heavy / wiki-like experience

The prototype should demonstrate a much more modern, maintainable, useful university digital experience.

ToolMind is a separate project.
DO NOT integrate ToolMind in this 5-day prototype.
ToolMind remains postponed and its original vision must remain intact.
